A postmortem, not a verdict.

Companies fail through a chain of choices, constraints, and timing. We separate records from interpretation so readers can disagree with the diagnosis.

  1. Collect

    Prefer filings, company statements, and contemporaneous reporting.

  2. Reconstruct

    Put funding, product shifts, layoffs, and shutdowns back in order.

  3. Separate

    Label verified facts, disputed claims, and editorial inference.

  4. Revise

    Keep each file open to corrections and stronger evidence.
